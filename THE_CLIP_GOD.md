# PART 1: THE MINDSET

## 1.1 What You Actually Do

You do not film. You do not appear on camera. You do not need charisma.

You find content that is **already interesting** and **package it better** than anyone else.

Your brand = your taste + your consistency. Viewers follow you because you surface great moments reliably.

## 1.2 The Viral Equation

```
VIRALITY = PACKAGING × HOOK × RETENTION × SHARE TRIGGER
```

Each term **multiplies**. A zero anywhere kills the whole result.

- ZERO packaging = nobody clicks
- ZERO hook = they leave in 2 seconds
- ZERO retention = algorithm buries you
- ZERO share trigger = no organic spread

## 1.3 The Three Levers You Control

1. **Niche selection** — the single most important decision
2. **Speed to publish** — first clip of a viral moment wins
3. **Posting consistency** — algorithm rewards reliability

# PART 2: NICHE SELECTION

## 2.1 The Rule

> Pick ONE niche and dominate it. Do NOT be a general clip channel.

A general channel posts Hasanabi Monday, Joe Rogan Tuesday, Valorant Wednesday → the algorithm gets 3 conflicting signals and shows your content to nobody.

A niche channel posts **one topic 7 days a week** → the algorithm knows exactly who to target. 5-10x more views per clip.

## 2.2 The Best Niches for CLIPGODCLIP

| Niche | Clippability | Competition | Monetization | Best For |
|-------|-------------|-------------|--------------|----------|
| **Kick IRL / Just Chatting** | Very High | LOW | Medium | 🔥 BEST OVERALL |
| **Finance/Business Podcasts** | Medium | Low-Medium | VERY HIGH ($) | Highest RPM |
| **Twitch Gaming (specific game)** | High | High | Medium | Saturated |
| **VTubers** | High | Low-Medium | Medium | Engaged audience |

## 2.3 How to Choose

Pick the niche where **all 3** are true:

1. **Abundant source material** — 5-10+ creators producing weekly content you can clip
2. **Low competition** — fewer than 5 established clip channels above 50K subs
3. **You actually enjoy watching it** — you'll burn out otherwise

## 2.4 The Live Clipping Advantage

Live streamers (especially on Kick) are the **single best opportunity** because:

- Constant unscripted moments (every few minutes, not once an hour)
- You can clip and post **while the stream is still live** — search interest spiking
- VOD clippers can't compete on timing
- Kick is massively **under-clipped** compared to Twitch

# PART 3: THE HOOK

## 3.1 Why It Matters

65% of viewers leave in the first 2 seconds. The hook decides whether anyone sees the rest.

## 3.2 The 3-Second Hook Formula

```
PATTERN INTERRUPT + SPECIFIC PROMISE + CREDIBILITY
```

## 3.3 Proven Hook Templates

| Template | Example |
|----------|---------|
| **The Contrarian** | "Everything you've been told about [X] is wrong" |
| **The Curiosity Gap** | "There's one trick [experts] don't want you to know" |
| **The Outcome** | "I went from 0 to 100K in 90 days — here's how" |
| **The Action Start** | Start mid-sentence, mid-reaction, mid-drama |
| **The Specific Number** | "3 signs your [X] is failing" |
| **The Mistake Reveal** | "I chased virality for 6 months and lost everything" |
| **The Industry Secret** | "This is what [industry] doesn't tell you" |

Rule: Write the hook BEFORE you clip anything. If the hook doesn't work, nothing else matters.

# PART 4: THUMBNAILS

## 4.1 What the Data Says (vidIQ study of 500 breakout videos)

- **Face in thumbnail:** 69% of all breakouts, 75% of top 100, **80% of top 50**
- **High contrast:** 56% of breakouts (baseline expectation)
- **Face OR contrast:** 89% of all breakouts
- **Text:** 28% used NO text. 24% used 1-3 words. Median = 5 words.
- **Exaggerated expression:** Only 5% — genuine expressions win

## 4.2 The 6 Rules

1. **One clear subject** — face filling 40-60% of frame
2. **High contrast colors** — bright subject, dark background (cyan/orange, yellow/purple)
3. **3 words max** — bold, sans-serif, readable at 120px wide
4. **Curiosity gap** — show the BEFORE not the payoff
5. **Design at phone size first** — test at 156×88px
6. **Title + thumbnail = one unit** — thumbnail shows, title tells. Never repeat each other.

## 4.3 Thumbnail CTR Benchmarks

| Source | Average | Good | Excellent |
|--------|---------|------|-----------|
| YouTube Search | 8-10% | 10-12% | 12-15%+ |
| Browse (Home) | 3-5% | 5-7% | 7%+ |
| Suggested | 5-7% | 7-9% | 9.5%+ |

# PART 5: RETENTION & STRUCTURE

## 5.1 The Micro-Story Structure

Every clip follows this:

```
HOOK (0-3s) → CONTEXT (3-10s) → PAYOFF (10-60s) → CTA (end)
```

## 5.2 Retention Rules

- **Target 70%+ completion rate** — this is the TikTok algorithm threshold
- **Add a visual change every 4-5 seconds** — zoom, text pop, scene switch
- **Cut every slow moment** — if it's boring to you, cut it
- **Lead with the payoff** — show the best part first, THEN give context

## 5.3 Optimal Clip Length

| Platform | Best Length | Why |
|----------|------------|-----|
| TikTok | 60-90 seconds | 60s minimum for Creator Rewards payout |
| YouTube Shorts | 15-45 seconds | Shorter = higher completion rate |
| Instagram Reels | 15-60 seconds | Peak performance |

## 6.2 The 80/10/10 Mix

- **80% individual clips** — your bread and butter
- **10% compilations** — "Best of [creator] this week"
- **10% trend-jacking** — tie your niche to trending audio/topics

## 6.3 Source Rotation

Use 3-5 source channels and rotate weekly. This:
- Prevents over-clipping one creator (copyright risk)
- Keeps content variety high
- Builds relationships with multiple creators

## 6.4 Batching Workflow

1. **Pick clips** — 30 minutes, scroll through source content, mark timestamps
2. **Process** — your repurpose.py tool (download, crop, caption)
3. **Review** — 15 minutes, check quality
4. **Schedule** — queue for the week

Total: ~1 hour/day for 3 clips across 2 platforms

# PART 8: PLATFORM STRATEGY

## 8.1 TikTok (Fastest Growth)

- Algorithm actively shows content to non-followers
- Best discovery engine for new channels
- Post clips 60-90 seconds (for CRP eligibility)
- Hook in first 2 seconds or they scroll
- Use trending sounds when relevant
- Engage with every comment in the first hour

## 8.2 YouTube Shorts (Best Long-Term)

- Videos can be discovered through search **months later** (evergreen)
- Better monetization path (Shorts → long-form → full YPP)
- Post 15-45 second clips here
- Thumbnails matter more on YouTube than TikTok
- Use titles with SEO keywords

## 8.3 Instagram Reels

- Audience skews older, higher disposable income
- 30-50% of TikTok performance for clipper content
- Worth running in parallel (same clip, different caption)
- Save/repost culture helps spread

## 8.4 Cross-Platform Rules

- **NEVER include watermarks** — every platform suppresses competitor watermarks
- Same clip, **different caption** per platform
- Post at **different times** per platform (peak hours differ)
- **Same handle** everywhere for brand consistency

# PART 11: THE PSYCHOLOGY OF SHAREABILITY

People share content for **7 reasons**. Stack 3+ per clip:

| Trigger | What It Means |
|---------|--------------|
| **Social Currency** | Sharing makes them look smart/early/in-the-know |
| **Identity Signal** | "That's SO me" — tag a friend energy |
| **High Arousal** | Awe, excitement, anger (NOT sadness/calm) |
| **Practical Value** | Saves time, money, or effort |
| **Curiosity Gap** | Information feels incomplete without clicking |
| **Relationship Utility** | Makes them think of a specific person |
| **Narrative** | Story triggers neural coupling — brain syncs |

## The Arousal Hierarchy

```
AWE → HUMOR → ANGER → SURPRISE → ANXIETY → (gap) → SADNESS → CONTENTMENT
```

High arousal = shares. Low arousal = silence.

# PART 12: YOUR TOOL

Your `scripts/repurpose.py` handles the production pipeline:

```
python scripts/repurpose.py "https://youtube.com/..." --hashtags "fyp viral clips" --clip-duration 45 --output output
```

**What it does:**
1. Downloads video from any URL (YT, TikTok, IG, Twitter, etc.)
2. Transcribes with faster-whisper (free, local, no API key)
3. Splits into clips (default ~45s)
4. Crops to 1080×1920 vertical 9:16
5. Hard-burns captions into the frames
6. Outputs ready-to-upload .mp4 files + hashtags.txt

## 13.3 Content Publishing APIs (Post Clips Automatically)

### TikTok Content Posting API

- **Endpoint:** `POST /v2/post/publish/video/init/`
- **OAuth scope:** `video.publish`
- **Rate limit:** 6 requests/min per token, ~15-25 posts/day per account
- **Video:** MP4, max 1GB, 3s-10min, 9:16 recommended
- **Key requirement:** App must pass **TikTok audit** to post public videos (2-6 weeks review). Unaudited apps = private posts only.

### YouTube Data API v3

- **Upload cost:** ~100 quota units (reduced from 1,600 in Dec 2025!)
- **Daily quota:** 10,000 units → ~100 uploads/day default
- **OAuth:** Required for uploads. Access token 1 hour, refresh token long-lived.
- **Key requirement:** App must be in "production" mode (not testing) for long-lived refresh tokens.

### Instagram Graph API

- **Post limit:** 25 posts/24 hours per Business/Creator account
- **Rate limit:** 200 calls/hour per account
- **OAuth:** Meta App Review required (weeks). Personal accounts blocked.

### Unified Posting APIs (Post to multiple platforms from one call)
